Setting up and playing a turn

Set up an episode

Do these in order before the first turn (pp. 66-71).

1
Determine the episode

Roll 1D6 or pick one (pp. 126-132).

1 The Battle2 The Escape3 The Race4 The Raid5 The Rescue6 The Theft
2
Attacker and defender

Each side rolls 1D6 + the highest Mind in its cast. The higher result chooses to attack or defend.

3
Tropes optional

Add a trope (18 on p. 145). Tropes often change the number and placing of objective tokens, so check first.

4
Objective tokens
  • Roll 1D6 for how many tokens.
  • The defender places the first face down, then sides alternate.
  • Not within 12" of a start location or 6" of another token.
  • To collect: base contact and a special action. A Star or Co-Star who claims one gains ★★.
5
The maguffin optional

The token furthest from any model. Whoever claims it draws a random maguffin card (use once, then discard). Worth 1D3 VP.

6
Start locations

The defender picks a table edge; the attacker takes the opposite one. Each start zone runs from the edge up to 3" in.

7
Place your models

Defender, then attacker, place models without Espionage. Then defender, then attacker, place Espionage models. Keep units together.

8
Build the script deck

Shuffle each act. Stack finale cards at the bottom, act two on them, act one on top.

No script cards? Use clubs, spades and diamonds (p. 76).

9
Prop store

1 prop card per Star and Co-Star, +1 per model with the Prop special effect, plus any bonus cards from other effects.

10
Roll for initiative

Each side rolls 1D6 + the highest Mind in its cast. A tie: both gain ★ and roll again. The winner is on screen first; the other side gains ★★.

Turn sequence

One side is on screen each turn and plays phases 1 to 3. Then the other side takes its turn.

1
Script phase

Flip 1 card (1 or 2 after your first turn). Flipping 2 gives both sides ★★; play the last card only.

2
Action phase

Gain plot points, activate models, remove statuses.

3
End phase
  • Axed? More models removed from play than in play: pass a Spirit test with a Star or Co-Star in play, or remove 1D6 models. Axed Extras stop generating ★.
  • On Fire? Roll for each model (page 2).
  • Steal the scene? Once per episode: activate one Star or Co-Star again. Not once a finale card is on top of the deck.

In action

Activating models

Pay ★ to activate a model. It gets two actions. It cannot make two strikes (fight, shoot or presence) in one activation, or a fight and a shoot.

Move
  • Up to 6" across clear ground.
  • Most models can take both actions as moves.
  • Any move can change facing.
Strike
  • Fight: brawl, blunt or bladed weapon.
  • Shoot: a ranged weapon.
  • Presence: wits or strength of will.
Special
  • Aim.
  • Pick up an objective token.
  • Some special effects.
Free
  • Use a prop.
  • Charge attack.
  • Use a star quality.
  • Some special effects.

A special effect's card says whether it needs a special action or is free.

Stretch dice

Buy extra dice for a defence roll, strike roll or statistic test: ★ per die, bought before you roll.

Score = your highest die, +1 for each other die that rolled 4, 5 or 6.
Rolls 2, 4 and 5 score 5 + 1 = 6.

Disadvantaged: roll the initial die only, no stretch dice. Rerolls: keep the second result.

Statuses

  • -1 to strike and defence rolls for each status.
  • A model has each status once. Gaining one it already has costs 1 Health instead.
  • In your action phase, ★ removes one status from one model (not Poisoned).
Captured

Generates no ★; its captor gains them. To escape: activate it, pay ★ and win an opposed Spirit test (disadvantaged).

Distracted

Reroll the highest die on every roll. A second move needs a passed Mind test.

Dominated

Joins the other cast. Removing it costs ★ and needs an ally in base contact.

Immobilised

Cannot activate until removed. Cannot be removed while it has other statuses. Disadvantaged.

Infected

Plot points cannot remove it. At 0 Health the enemy rolls 1D6 in their end phase: on a 1 it returns as a new profile in their cast.

On Fire

In your end phase roll 2D6: equal to or above Defence loses 1 Health. Then the enemy may move it up to 3".

Poisoned

★★ to remove.

Scared

No presence attacks. A fight or shoot attack needs a passed Spirit test, or its strike counts as half (round up).

Stunned

Knocked down. Cannot be given ★ in the action phase or make any attack, free strikes included. Rolls no dice when defending.

Weakened

Only one action when activated.

About Casting Office

Casting Office is a free, unofficial tool for designing profile cards and building casts for 7TV 2nd Edition, the cinematic skirmish game by Crooked Dice Game Design Studio. Start from any of the 229 official Core and 80s profiles, customise it under the rules on pp. 38-47 of the Core Rulebook, or design your own Stars, Co-Stars, Extras and vehicles from scratch.

The card layout follows the official 2nd Edition cards so your custom profiles sit alongside the real ones on the table.

Get the official game

7TV, its rulebooks, genre guides and profile card art belong to Crooked Dice. You need the books to play, and this tool is no substitute for them. Rulebooks, profile cards, miniatures, tokens and scenery are all on the Crooked Dice website.

Good to know

  • Your cards and casts are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded. Use Export backup in My Cards to keep a copy or move to another computer.
  • Cards print at 63.5 x 88 mm, the size of standard card sleeves. Print the PDF at 100% (actual size).
  • Headings use League Gothic, numbers Anton and card text PT Sans Narrow, free stand-ins for the fonts on the printed cards. Frames and icons are drawn by the app, apart from the genre symbols.
  • The ratings calculator applies the customisation rules as written. Agree custom profiles with your opponent before play, as the rulebook recommends.

Errata

The official profiles in this tool include these corrections, confirmed by Karl Perrotto of Crooked Dice on 24 July 2026:

  • Grizzled Veteran (Core): the Quickdraw special effect is Power Behind The Throne P. Allies' Leader or Unit Leader special effects are extended by 3" for this turn.
  • Hardened Grunt (80s): has Military Training P and no Unit Leader. When this model makes a strike, it can choose to add +1 to its strike roll for every plot point spent on extra dice, instead of getting the extra dice.
